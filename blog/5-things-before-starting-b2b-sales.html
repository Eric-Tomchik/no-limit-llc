5 Things I Wish I Knew Before Starting in B2B Sales

July 2026 · 5 min read · By the No Limit Team

Starting a career in B2B sales can feel like jumping into the deep end. Whether you're coming from retail, food service, another industry, or straight out of school — here's what we wish someone had told us before day one.

1. Your Attitude Matters More Than Your Experience

Seriously. The most successful sales reps at No Limit didn't walk in with years of experience. They walked in with hunger, coachability, and a refusal to quit. Skills can be taught. Mindset can't.

If you're the kind of person who takes feedback, learns quickly, and doesn't let rejection slow you down — you already have the most important qualification.

2. Rejection Is Part of the Game — Not a Reflection of You

In B2B sales, you'll hear "no" more than "yes." That's just math. The best reps don't take it personally — they see every "no" as one step closer to the next "yes."

The difference between a good rep and a great rep isn't talent — it's how they handle the 9 no's before the 1 yes.

Once you internalize this, the job gets a lot more fun. Every door becomes an opportunity, not a threat.

3. Consistency Beats Intensity

Everyone can have a great day. What separates top performers is that they show up and execute every single day. Five solid deals a week, consistently, is worth more than one incredible week followed by two mediocre ones.

At No Limit, that's actually our benchmark — just 5 deals per week puts you at $1,000+ weekly. Consistency turns into compound growth, and before you know it, you're one of the top earners on the team.

4. The Team Around You Matters — A Lot

B2B sales isn't a solo sport. The team you train with, the leaders who mentor you, and the culture you're surrounded by have a massive impact on how fast you grow.

That's why finding the right company matters. Look for a team that actually invests in training, celebrates wins together, and promotes from within. (Hint: that's what we do at No Limit.)

5. Your Earning Potential Is Truly in Your Hands

In most jobs, your paycheck is decided by someone else. In B2B sales — especially 100% commission — you decide your income based on how hard you work and how smart you sell.

That can feel scary at first, but it's actually the most liberating thing about this career. There's no manager deciding you're worth a 3% raise. If you want to earn more, you go earn more. Period.

The Bottom Line

B2B sales isn't for everyone. But if you've got the drive, the coachability, and the ambition to bet on yourself — it might be the best decision you ever make. We've seen people go from zero experience to team leaders in under a year. The only question is: are you ready?
