Our Foundation

Our Company

No Limit LLC is a standalone business operating under Cornerstone Marketing Concepts, LLC (cmcgroups.com) — a nationwide customer acquisition firm that has been partnering with leading telecom and technology brands for over a decade.

Based in the Austin, Texas market, No Limit produces top-tier sales agents specializing in both the retention of existing business accounts and the acquisition of new ones. We represent 8+ major brands including AT&T Business, Comcast Business, Spectrum, T-Mobile, Frontier, Optimum, DirecTV, and Clover POS — delivering professional, results-driven sales solutions that businesses trust.

Built on performance and powered by people, No Limit combines CMC's proven sales systems, data-backed strategy, and a culture of leadership development to deliver consistent, high-quality results. Our commitment to professionalism, training, and accountability has positioned us as a premier team within the CMC network.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining No Limit LLC and building a career in B2B sales.

What type of position is this?

This is a 1099 independent contractor position. You'll operate as your own boss with the freedom and flexibility that comes with being an independent business professional. This means you control your schedule, your approach, and ultimately your income.

How does the compensation work?

Compensation is 100% commission-based with unlimited earning potential. There are absolutely no caps on what you can earn. Top-performing agents consistently earn well above traditional salaried positions. The more effort you put in, the more you make — it's that simple.

Do I need prior sales experience?

No experience is required. We provide comprehensive hands-on training from day one. Our proven sales methodology, product training, and mentorship program are designed to transform ambitious individuals — regardless of background — into elite B2B sales professionals.

What brands will I represent?

No Limit LLC is contracted with 8+ major brands including AT&T Business, Comcast Business, Spectrum, T-Mobile, Frontier, Optimum, DirecTV, and Clover POS. You'll be selling products and services that businesses genuinely need — making conversations easier and more impactful.

What does the training look like?

Training includes hands-on field experience, product knowledge sessions, sales methodology workshops, and one-on-one mentorship with experienced leaders. You'll learn proven techniques for B2B account retention and acquisition, objection handling, and closing strategies. Most new agents start producing results within their first week.

Is there room for advancement?

Absolutely. We promote from within. Our clear leadership track takes you from sales agent to team leader to management — all based on your performance and ambition. No Limit is part of Cornerstone Marketing Concepts, LLC, a nationwide organization with offices across the country, so the growth potential is truly unlimited.

Where is the position located?

No Limit LLC operates in the Austin, Texas market — one of the fastest-growing tech and business hubs in America. You'll be working in the field meeting with local businesses, backed by a supportive team and office environment.

How do I apply?

Simply fill out the application form above or schedule an interview directly. Our team will reach out within 24-48 hours to discuss next steps. The process is quick — we're looking for motivated individuals ready to start building their future.
